A daily bird game

Maydle: guess today’s Isle of May bird

Every day there is a new mystery bird from the Isle of May checklist. You have six guesses. Each guess tells you how close you are.

Today’s bird

    Your guesses and clues
    BirdOrderFamilyWingspanIsland statusLast recordedRecords committee

    How to play

    • Green a match (wingspan within 2 cm, same status level, same year, same committee)
    • Yellow close: same order but a different family, wingspan within about a fifth, one step away in status (for example Scarce and Uncommon), or within two years
    • Grey not close
    • ▲ ▼ the mystery bird is bigger or smaller, more or less common on the island, or was last recorded more or less recently

    Where the birds come from

    All 293 species on the observatory’s own checklist, Birds of the Isle of May (all records to the end of 2018), with its status, last year and records committee for each bird. The daily bird is never a vagrant or rare visitor, but you can guess any of the 293.

    Design note: an idea for the Trust, not part of the current site. Species, status, last year and records committee come from the Trust’s checklist (to the end of 2018); an updated checklist would bring it up to date. Order and family follow standard taxonomy. Wingspans are approximate typical figures from standard field guides, not from the Trust, and would need checking before launch. Scores are kept only in the player’s own browser.

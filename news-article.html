An island first: White-throated Sparrow

First seen 5 August 2026Updated 20 September 2026

News of an island first: a White-throated Sparrow in the Low Light bushes on 5 August 2026, which then stayed on and off into September.

Pics courtesy D. Steel and W. Cresswell.

05 Aug: WHITE-THROATED SPARROW (1st. for the Isle of May) in Low Light bushes remained all day.

The sparrow was found in the bushes by the Low Light, the former lighthouse that houses the observatory, and it remained all day. It was then reported in the log on many days through August and into September.

1stWhite-throated Sparrow ever recorded on the Isle of May.

From the log

  • 05 AugWHITE-THROATED SPARROW (1st for the Isle of May) in Low Light bushes remained all day.
  • 06 AugWhite-throated Sparrow still, Marsh Harrier 1 immature/female, Garden Warbler 1.
  • 12 AugWhite-throated Sparrow not seen today, Turnstone 139, Purple Sandpiper 71, Common Sandpiper 9.
  • 14 AugWhite-throated Sparrow still present, Wheatear 1, Willow Warbler 4, Common Sandpiper 1.
  • 16 AugWhite-throated Sparrow not seen today, SE Owl 2, Teal 1.
  • 23 AugSooty Shearwater 3, Manx 19, Bonxie 3, Arctic Skua 3, Gadwall 1, White-throated Sparrow 1, still present.
  • 16 Sep… 1 Osprey, 1 Puffin, 1 Pomarine Skua, 1 White-throated Sparrow still present.
  • 20 SepSabine’s Gull today and White-throated Sparrow remains skulking.

Compiled from the observatory’s Bird News log and homepage updates, August and September 2026.

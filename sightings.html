Reading the log

Species in CAPITALS are scarce or rare on the island. N, S, E and W give the direction of birds flying past. PF Goose and Pinkfeet are Pink-footed Geese; Bonxie is Great Skua; SE Owl is Short-eared Owl.

Rare birds

Records committees

Rare and scarce birds recorded on the island are subject to consideration by the relevant records committees to ensure that the island list is properly validated. These include the BBRC (opens in a new tab), the SBRC (opens in a new tab) and the island’s own Isle of May RC.

Groups visiting are advised to familiarise themselves with the lists to ensure they submit sightings details as part of essential record keeping. Guidance on submissions is available in the Low Light.

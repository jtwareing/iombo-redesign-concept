Visiting the island

To visit the Isle of May National Nature Reserve as a day visitor, boat trips are available from several operators:

  • From Anstruther: www.isleofmayboattrips.co.uk (opens in a new tab) and www.isleofmayferry.com (opens in a new tab)
  • From North Berwick and Dunbar: seabird.org/boats (opens in a new tab) and bluewild.co.uk (opens in a new tab)

More on visiting the reserve is on the NatureScot website (opens in a new tab).

Charity details

The Isle of May Bird Observatory and Field Station Trust is a Scottish Charitable Incorporated Organisation, Scottish Charity Number SC001783. View our entry on the Scottish Charity Register (opens in a new tab).

Donations and Support

Please support us by joining as a member or donating

The Observatory is run by a management committee on a voluntary basis. Your membership fee or donation is a valuable contribution which supports the operation of the Observatory.

The Mission Statement of the Isle of May Bird Observatory

The Isle of May Bird Observatory facilitates the monitoring and study of bird migration and other aspects of ornithology and natural history, by anyone with an interest in the natural world. In conjunction with partners, it contributes to bird conservation and science through its activities, it provides opportunities for ornithological training and education for all, in safe and welcoming facilities, at events and through the media. It enables enjoyment of, and participation in, the study of the natural world.

Become a member of the Isle of May Bird Observatory Trust

The annual subscription for a member of the Trust is just £25.

  • An excellent annual report (printed and/or pdf)
  • Two newsletters (emailed pdf)
  • Booking priority during the members booking period
  • Members may attend member events and the AGM

Not least, your membership fee is a valuable contribution which supports the operation of the Observatory.

Donate to support the observatory

If you would like to make a donation to the observatory you can do so online. Choose an amount below, or pick “Other amount” to give any sum you like, securely online.

Design note: on the live site, “Other amount” opens one more Stripe payment link, set to customers choose what to pay, so a donor can type any amount and give online straight away, with no form or email needed. The Treasurer can create it in the Stripe dashboard in a few minutes (Payment Links, then New, then “Customers choose what to pay”), with an optional suggested amount and minimum. It pays into the same Stripe account as the other four buttons.

Each amount opens a secure Stripe payment page in a new tab.

Design note: in the live build a short line here could say what each amount helps with (for example rings, trap repairs or the Low Light), once the Trust has chosen the examples it would like to use.

Larger gifts and specific initiatives

Any amount can now be given online with “Other amount” above. To discuss a larger gift or support a specific initiative, contact the Honorary Secretary of the IOMBO Trust.

Past projects made possible by grants and donors include the renewal of the Bain Trap in 2019 and the repair of the Low Light chimneys in 2025.
